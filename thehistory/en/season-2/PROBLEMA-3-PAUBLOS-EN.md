## There are events that change physics

There are events that change history.

There are events that change physics.

And there are events that make physics request a transfer to another department.

**THE PROBLEM OF THE 3 PAUBLOS** belongs to the third category.

---

## Tuesday that had already gone wrong

It all began on a Tuesday that had already gone wrong before it was born.

At [QEL@0xpblab](/qel), the clocks were showing different times for no reason.

The coffee machine was serving espresso, sugarcane juice and a liquid identified only as "liquid shame".

The elevator in the Ontological Synchronisation Tower kept stopping on the same floor, even though the building swore, in official documentation, that it did not have that floor.

**[Pablo Mu-R4d](/pablo)**, president, CEO and permanent chronological aberration, was in his 38th consecutive year of being 38.

Seated in his command chair, he was reviewing reports of the kind:

- TIME LEAK IN THE BASEMENT
- EMPLOYEE TRAPPED IN WEDNESDAY
- PENGUIN FROM THE CRYOQUANTIC DIVISION SPEAKING LATIN AGAIN
- WHO AUTHORISED A JAM SESSION IN THE CONTAINMENT SECTOR

---

## Maximum Irresponsibility Alarm

It was at that exact moment that the **Maximum Irresponsibility Alarm** went off.

It did not sound like a siren.

It sounded like the soundtrack of a film in which every important person is about to make a terrible decision.

The central screen in the auditorium flickered and displayed:

```txt
DETECTION OF THREE NARRATIVE GRAVITY CENTRES
CONFLICTING PHONETIC IDENTITY
COLLAPSE RISK: PAUBLO-HIGH
TECHNICAL NOTE: OH NO, NOT AGAIN
```

"Explain in plain language", ordered Pablo.

The risk analyst, with eye bags the size of failed dimensions, replied:

> *"Sir, there are three entities with sufficient dramatic mass to deform reality using the same approximate semantic field."*  
> *"Translate."*  
> *"There's too much Paulo in the system."*

And it was true.

---

## Too much Paulo in the system

At some point in the continuum, three forces had begun to converge:

### 1. PABLO MU-R4D

Owner of QEL@0xpblab.

Executive of impossible science.

The man who tries to run reality as if it were a spreadsheet with passive-aggressive fury.

His greatest power was not technology.

It was saying "let's align expectations" in a way that made even black holes reconsider their priorities.

### 2. PAULO PA-MONHA

Embodied resentment with the wrong overcoats.

Terror of interdimensional streaming.

Future villain of shamefully apocalyptic scale.

A man so petty he turned "I won't give you my password" into ideological fuel to destroy an entire dimension.

His [poor army](/paulo-pa-monha) still had no soldiers, but had pure bad intention.

### 3. PAULO PIRATA

Old, lame, rocker, biker, highway bandit and owner of the most permanently dusty nose in the universe of [Pó-Blanc](/paulo-pirata).

A combination of pirate captain, guitar solo, bad carburation and emotional threat.

If the wind heard the engine of the Widow of Lime, it changed direction out of respect or self-preservation.

---

## The three centres of narrative gravity

Apart, the three were already a mistake.

Together, they were the metaphysical equivalent of putting three drunk DJs at the helm of the same submarine during an eclipse.

---

## Liu-Kikuchi-Moretti Formula

The Department of Bureaucratic Astrophysics immediately applied the **Liu-Kikuchi-Moretti Formula for Convergent Ego Systems**.

The result was:

```txt
????????
```

followed by

```txt
NO MATHEMATICS EXISTS FOR THIS
```

followed by

```txt
PLEASE CONSULT A PRIEST, A SCREENWRITER OR AN EXORCIST
```

**Dr Célia de Moura**, head of the Department of Embarrassing Cosmology, tried to explain the situation in scientific language:

> *"In the three-body problem, the motion of three masses subject only to mutual gravitation tends to chaotic behaviour and has no simple general solution. Here we have three narrative masses subject not only to gravity but to ego, spite, leadership, bike, cursed remote control, rock, grudge and budget. In technical terms: we're screwed."*

Pablo took a deep breath.

That was the most a metaphysical CEO could do before starting to break things.

> *"What's the distance between them?"*  
> *"Geometric, causal or moral?"*  
> *"The worst one."*  
> *"They're practically touching."*

The whole room fell silent.

Even the printer stopped, perhaps out of compassion.

---

## The sky opens like one tab too many

Then the sky above QEL@0xpblab opened.

Not like a portal.

Not like a rift.

Not like a tear.

It opened like **one tab too many in the universe's browser**.

The firmament became a cracked screen.

The sun appeared three times.

The moon went on medical leave.

A cloud took the exact shape of a streaming platform manager saying "this content is not available in your cosmic region".

In the lab's central courtyard, staff ran back and forth with the elegance of disaster-movie extras who had been told to look busy but not useful.

One intern asked if they needed to evacuate the building.

Another replied:

> *"Depends. Evacuate to where?"*

The first looked at the tripled sky.

> *"Good point."*

---

## Arrival of the three

First came **Pablo**, in the courtyard itself, because he was already there and that gave him an irritating logistical advantage.

Then the air began to smell of burnt plastic, leaked password and greenhouse-cultivated hatred.

The monitors showed buffering.

The intercoms played the same phrase in twenty-two languages and one offensive accent:

> *"Subscription expired."*

It was **[Paulo Pa-Monha](/paulo-pa-monha)**.

He emerged from inside an elevator that did not belong to the building, wrapped in smoke so over the top it looked like it had been hired just for him.

He entered with his army of nobody, represented that day by:

- a shopping cart wheel
- a crooked banner reading "WAR"
- a plastic bag with more stage presence than many billion-dollar franchise actors

> *"Pablo!", shouted Paulo Pa-Monha, as if the first syllable were a stab.*  
> *"Paulo", replied Pablo, with that dangerous calm of an executive who has already fired more elegant anomalies.*  
> *"Don't use my name with that corporate-tutorial tone."*  
> *"You invaded my dimension."*  
> *"I came to demand justice."*  
> *"You came to complain about login."*  
> *"LOGIN IS CIVILISATION."*

Before the argument could grow to the healthy level of a silly war, a roar was heard in the distance.

Not thunder.

Not an engine.

A mechanical insult.

From the white horizon of a newly opened dimensional corridor came **The Widow of Lime**, spitting dust, sparks, blasphemy and a guitar solo that seemed to have run over good taste and reversed for sport.

On the bike, limping even while seated, came **[Paulo Pirata](/paulo-pirata)**.

Cracked jacket.

Nose eternally dirty with white dust.

Broken glasses.

The expression of a man who accepts no advice, rule, conciliation or detergent.

---

## Which of you two is the wrong Paulo

He stopped the bike on top of the official QEL@0xpblab symbol.

Looked one way.

Looked the other.

Scratched his nose with his thumb.

And said the phrase that almost destroyed the phonetics of the universe:

> *"Which of you two is the wrong Paulo?"*

The silence that followed was so absolute that in another galaxy someone heard it and felt uncomfortable without knowing why.

The lab's central system went into panic.

The panel displayed:

```txt
IMPOSSIBLE TO RESOLVE IDENTITY
PAULO DETECTED
PAULO DETECTED
PABLO DETECTED
AUTOMATIC CORRECTION: PAUBLO, PAUBLO AND PAUBLO
GOD HAS ABANDONED US OR IS LAUGHING
```

---

## The Triple Paublification

There was born the phenomenon that chroniclers would call **The Triple Paublification**.

When three figures with sufficient authority over chaos share a phonetically confusing name, reality tries to save processing.

It merges references.

Mixes pronouns.

Swaps blame.

Combines motivations.

And delivers to the cosmos a new and deeply offensive conceptual object: the **PAUBLO**.

The first symptom was linguistic.

Every time someone shouted "Paulo!", all three replied "WHAT?" at once, producing an echo so aggressive it knocked out two windows, one doctoral thesis and the self-esteem of a quantum HR manager.

The second symptom was gravitational.

The presence field of the three began to deform the courtyard.

The tiles rippled as if they were watching a Michael Jackson show and trying to learn the moonwalk on their own.

A guard slid backwards unintentionally, perfectly in line, pointed at his own feet and whispered:

> *"That wasn't me. It was pop physics."*

The third symptom was cultural.

References began to leak into the world without asking permission.

The canteen turned into the Mos Eisley cantina for seventeen seconds.

A corridor took on Blade Runner aesthetics but without the budget.

The legal department was possessed by the spirit of The Office and began producing minutes in documentary-cringe format.

The basement went Stranger Things mode, only with corporate mould.

A door opened onto a set that looked like a mix of Mad Max, El Chavo and Doctor Who, which no one had known was possible and, after seeing it, continued to think it shouldn't be.

Paulo Pa-Monha pointed his colossal remote control at Paulo Pirata.

> *"Identify yourself, lame stranger."*

Paulo Pirata took a toothpick from the corner of his mouth and replied:

> *"You identify yourself, apocalyptic rental-store scarecrow."*  
> *"I am the harbinger of the end of this dimension."*  
> *"You look like the harbinger of an overdue bill."*  
> *"I will destroy everything Pablo built."*  
> *"Sure, but first learn to enter a place without looking like a bad themed party."*

The technicians began taking notes.

Not by method.

Out of documentary despair.

Pablo intervened:

> *"Enough. No one is destroying the dimension today."*

Paulo Pa-Monha turned towards him:

> *"I am."*

Paulo Pirata added:

> *"Maybe me too, depending on the soundtrack."*

Pablo closed his eyes for a second.

That was the closest he had ever come to requesting retirement from the universe.

---

## DONA EUNICE-7 and the Protocol

The ground trembled.

Not with anger.

With indecision.

QEL@0xpblab did not know whom to obey.

On one side, Pablo, administrative authority.

On another, Paulo Pa-Monha, authority of cosmic spite.

On the other other, Paulo Pirata, authority of aesthetic punch and loud exhaust.

Three centres.

Three egos.

Three distinct ways of ruining an environment.

And it was then that the Observatory of Dramatic Mechanics detected what they called **Paublographic Resonance**.

The signatures of the three went into interference.

Probabilities began to dance diagonally.

Light grew jealous of shadow.

Time tried to move forward, saw who was in front, and decided to turn back.

A hologram appeared in the middle of the courtyard.

It had the form of a teacher too tired for existence.

It was Protocol Intelligence, codename **DONA EUNICE-7**.

> *"Attention", she said, in a tone that mixed GPS, school principal and disciplinary threat. "According to the Anthroponymic Singularity Protocol, when three phonetically similar entities enter direct conflict, the universe must choose one of the following options: A) implode; B) divide the name into syllables and redistribute them; C) convene a conciliatory dance; D) call Michael Jackson."*

Everyone fell silent.

> *"Sorry, what?", asked Pablo.*  
> *DONA EUNICE-7 repeated: "Call Michael Jackson."*  
> *"That's part of the protocol?"*  
> *"Unfortunately, yes."*  
> *"Who wrote that?"*  
> *"The cultural contingency unit, during a conference that clearly should have ended earlier."*

Paulo Pa-Monha widened his eyes.

> *"I am not participating in choreographic mediation."*

Paulo Pirata gave a dry laugh.

> *"Coward."*

Pablo rubbed his forehead.

> *"There is no Michael Jackson operationally available for this type of meeting."*

---

## Operation Ontological Thriller

The system thought for three seconds, which was already too long for bad ideas.

Then it opened a classified file.

```txt
FILE: OPERATION ONTOLOGICAL THRILLER
STATUS: UNAUTHORISED
ORIGIN: 1987 NEW YEAR'S PARTY THAT GOT OUT OF HAND
```

Before anyone could cancel, the courtyard went dark.

A white spotlight descended from the tripled sky.

An impossible beat began to pound.

And from the mist emerged not exactly Michael Jackson, but a **highly illegal memorial-kinetic projection**, built from:

- cursed VHS tapes
- three pirated music videos
- choreographic data stolen from the collective unconscious
- and an HDMI cable of ethically dubious provenance

The projection raised a gleaming finger.

The hat came down.

The shoe shone.

And the entire lab understood, with the pure terror reserved for the great moments of art, that the universe was going to resolve that impasse on the basis of the **moonwalk**.

The first to be affected was the floor.

It lost moral grip.

The second was time.

Each step backwards produced six minutes forwards and one retroactive humiliation.

The third was Paulo Pa-Monha, who tried to resist but was pushed back by a choreographic wave so precise that his overcoat did an involuntary spin and became ridiculous in a new way, which had seemed impossible.

Paulo Pirata, for his part, saw it and said:

> *"Finally a protocol with respect for performance."*

He switched on the guitar.

Joined the beat without asking permission.

Played over it as if Billie Jean had landed in a roadside bar run by biker ghosts.

---

## Paublar Singularity Choreography

Pablo realised too late.

The projection, the guitar, the grudge and administrative authority began to synchronise.

DONA EUNICE-7 shouted:

> *"NO! DON'T FORM A QUARTET!"*

Too late.

The combination of:

- ontological moonwalk,
- moto-rock solo from Pó-Blanc,
- Paulo Pa-Monha's eschatological spite,
- and executive management in end-times suit

produced an effect never before foreseen by serious science or shameless science: **PAUBLAR SINGULARITY CHOREOGRAPHY**.

The tripled sky began to spin like a cosmic motel fan.

The three suns formed an apocalyptic boy-band alignment.

The poor army's banner tried to dance on its own.

The Widow of Lime revved while stationary.

The ownerless elevator opened and closed to the beat.

The telepathic fish from the cryogenic sector began to shout "hee-hee" in Sanskrit.

The entire QEL@0xpblab was pulled into a zone of impossible solution.

There was no more dispute.

No more negotiation.

Only spectacle bad enough to become great.

The three stared at each other.

**PABLO**, with the fury of someone who would have to explain this in a meeting.

**PAULO PA-MONHA**, with the hatred of someone who still hasn't got over the denied password.

**PAULO PIRATA**, with the aggressive calm of someone who would run over a court if the court got in the way of the bike.

> *"So that's it?", asked Pablo. "Reality is going to fall because of similar names?"*

DONA EUNICE-7 replied:

> *"Technically, because of similar names, incompatible egos and very strong aesthetic choices."*

Paulo Pa-Monha took a step forward:

> *"I propose we resolve this like adults."*  
> *"With talk?", asked a technician, too hopeful to survive.*  
> *"No", he said. "With sabotage."*

Paulo Pirata pointed his thumb at himself.

> *"I propose a race."*  
> *"A race of what?", asked Pablo.*  
> *"Of everything. Bike, ego, volume, catchphrase, resistance to one's own badness."*

Pablo took another deep breath.

By then he was breathing like someone negotiating with earthquakes.

Then he had an idea.

Horrible.

But less horrible than the rest.

> *"Neither", he said. "If this is the Problem of the 3 Paublos, we'll treat it as a systems problem."*  
> *"Speak properly", growled Paulo Pa-Monha.*  
> *"We'll find out which of us the universe can stand the longest without asking for a reset."*

---

## The Judgement of the Three Paublos

DONA EUNICE-7 immediately materialised the definitive test.

**THE JUDGEMENT OF THE THREE PAUBLOS.**

Three pulpits rose from the ground.

On each, a trial:

- an impossible form from the existence's fiscal department;
- a remote control with unlabelled buttons;
- a bike assembled from parts from dimensions that didn't speak to each other.

> *"The test is simple", explained DONA EUNICE-7. "Each must survive a natural challenge of the other."*

Pablo received the bike.

Paulo Pa-Monha received the form.

Paulo Pirata received the remote control.

> *"This is torture", said Pablo.*  
> *"This is art", corrected DONA EUNICE-7.*

Pablo tried to start the bike and was insulted by the carburation.

Paulo Pa-Monha faced the fiscal form and discovered, for the first time, a force superior to his own grudge.

Paulo Pirata pressed a button on the remote and accidentally turned the courtyard's gravity into a music-video track.

The judgement lasted seven minutes, twelve explosions, one union breakdown and two technically condemnable miracles.

In the end:

- Pablo managed to get the bike working, but it took him straight into a wall over philosophical disagreement;
- Paulo Pa-Monha set fire to half the form and was declared unfit for aesthetic bad faith;
- Paulo Pirata pressed "SUBTITLES: [TURN ON DAMN IT]" and opened Latin subtitles over all of reality, which solved nothing but looked good.

The system concluded:

```txt
NONE OF THE THREE IS ACCEPTABLE
REPEAT:
NONE OF THE THREE IS ACCEPTABLE
```

---

## Expulsion and Golden Rule

There was a respectful pause.

Even the wind felt ashamed.

Then, as a last attempt at self-preservation, the universe did the only sensible thing left: **it expelled the three from the centre of the equation**.

A semantic shock wave shattered the courtyard.

It didn't kill anyone.

It just redistributed dignity very unevenly.

Pablo was thrown back to the administrative tower, where he landed in his own chair with a memo already printed saying: *PLEASE NEVER REPEAT THIS*.

Paulo Pa-Monha was sucked into the ownerless elevator along with his poor army, his warrior plastic bag and two new grudges he promised to cultivate until they became international crime.

Paulo Pirata was spat back to Pó-Blanc on the Widow of Lime, crossing a white portal while shouting:

> *"THAT WAS A GOOD ONE! WHEN YOU HAVE ANOTHER DISASTER, CALL ME!"*

The memorial-kinetic projection did one last spin.

Played an impossibly elegant note.

Did a moonwalk over logic.

And disappeared.

The sky returned to normal.

That is: to the kind of normal that QEL@0xpblab considers acceptable in a report.

After the catastrophe, the Council of Ontological Damage met for fifty-two hours to assess what had happened.

The final report ran to 913 pages, but its executive summary was simple:

**THE PROBLEM OF THE 3 PAUBLOS** demonstrated that:

- reality cannot support an excess of protagonism with repeated phonetics;
- spite, leadership and road rock must not share the same courtyard without containment;
- cultural protocols written after 11pm should be banned;
- any system that includes moonwalk as a method of arbitration must be audited;
- and, above all, when there is too much Paulo in the same equation, mathematics leaves the room and spectacle takes control.

Since then, QEL@0xpblab has maintained the **Golden Rule of Anti-Paublation**:

never allow the simultaneous convergence of Pablo, Paulo Pa-Monha and Paulo Pirata on the same causal axis without:

1. medical team,
2. legal team,
3. cleaning team,
4. dimensional-insult translator,
5. and a sound technician with nerves of steel.

The event entered the official chronology as satirical disaster, musical incident and cosmological warning.

It also became known by other names:

- **The War of the Mal-Calibrated Homonyms**
- **The Crisis of the Stumbled Consonant**
- **The Day the Universe Moonwalked Backwards**
- and, in the most honest internal documents: **THE MOST EXPENSIVE CLOWNING IN RECENT HISTORY**

To this day, when a new employee asks why there is a sign in the central courtyard reading *"NO DANCING, GOVERNING, AVENGING AND ACCELERATING AT THE SAME TIME"*, no one answers with precision.

They just point at the sky.

At the tower.

At the white corridor of Pó-Blanc.

And at a faint black mark on the ground, left by the impossible shoe of that forbidden projection.

Then they say, in a low, reverent and tired tone:

> *"You weren't here for the Problem of the 3 Paublos. And, honestly, that was better for you."*
