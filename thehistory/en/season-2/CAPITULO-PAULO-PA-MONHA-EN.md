## 🌐 The camera opens on the sky

The camera opens on the sky of QEL@0xpblab.

There are no stars. There are **buffering icons**. Thousands of them. Spinning across the firmament as if the entire universe were trying to load in 144p on internet shared by 38 civilizations, three accounting ghosts and a Mesozoic-era router installed in the basement of reality.

A thunderclap tears the sky. But it isn’t the sound of a storm. It’s the unmistakable sound of **someone aggressively clicking "Skip intro"** on every timeline at once.

The lab towers shake.

---

## 🚨 Alarms and hostile approach

Alarms begin to blare. Not the standard alarm, which had already blown out during the [A-Temporal DISRUPTION](/disruptura-atemporal). No. Now an improvised recording plays of a desperate intern screaming:

> *"SOMEONE'S COMING. HE'S ANGRY. HE'S BADLY DRESSED. HE HAS THE ENERGY OF A COSMIC LAWSUIT."*

Cut to the **Ontological Stabilisation Auditorium**, where scientists, chronotechnicians, quantum-coffee staff and a **telepathic fish on a CLT contract** discuss the residual collapse of the causal line.

At the centre of the room, in front of a screen showing forty-two contradictory versions of yesterday, stands **[Pablo Mu-R4d](/pablo)**, with his usual expression of someone who has already seen the fabric of existence catch fire before lunch and still has to approve the budget.

A technician runs up to him, breathless.

> *"President, CEO and permanent age anomaly, we have a hostile approach."*

Pablo doesn’t even look.

> *"How hostile?"*

The technician swallows hard.

> *"Existential-threat hostile? Or passive-aggressive-comment-on-a-shared-doc hostile?"*

The technician checks the panel.

> *"Worse."*

Pablo looks up for the first time.

> *"Worse than passive-aggressive comment?"*  
> *"Yes, sir. Much worse."*

## 📑 Twenty-seven tabs of vengeance

Silence.

> *"He opened twenty-seven tabs of vengeance."*

The room freezes. A researcher drops her mug. The telepathic fish vomits a sudoku.

On the main screen an impossible readout appears:

```txt
ANOMALY SOURCE: UNKNOWN
CLASS: UNBEARABLE
RISK LEVEL: ADMINISTRATIVE, MORAL, AESTHETIC AND POSSIBLY PHYSICAL
PROBABLE ID: PAULO PA-MONHA
```

No one says that name. Not because it’s forbidden. But because it sounds like the name of someone who would step on their own shadow just to pick a fight with the light.

The power goes out. Comes back. Goes out. Comes back in French. Goes out again.

---

## 🚪 The mall elevator and the smoke

The north wall of the auditorium turns into an **abandoned mall elevator**. The doors open with a creak so dramatic it seems rehearsed by an exhausted, badly paid expressionist theatre troupe.

Inside: nothing. Just smoke. Lots of smoke. Too much smoke. Smoke at an offensive level. Smoke so over the top it stops seeming intimidating and starts seeming like logistical incompetence.

A voice echoes from the gloom:

> *"I have arrived."*

No one answers. The voice returns, more annoyed:

> *"I SAID: I HAVE ARRIVED."*

A scientist mutters:

> *"We heard you the first time."*

The smoke parts. And then he appears.

---

## 🎩 Paulo Pa-Monha appears

**Paulo Pa-Monha.**

Thin as a poorly revised theory. Tall as a calculation error no one wants to own. Wearing an overcoat stitched from scraps of quantum motel curtains, cursed VHS sleeves and the inner lining of seats ripped from interdimensional buses.

* Around his neck, a **chain with several remote controls** hung like war trophies.
* On his left shoulder, a **dismantled mechanical parrot** that only knows how to say *"incorrect password"*.
* On his right, a piece of **satellite dish** used as a shoulder pad.

His hair looks like it was combed by a vengeful fan. His gaze has the sweetness of an overdue bill. He walks in with the conviction of someone who respects neither protocol, common sense, architecture nor anyone else’s dignity.

Every step he takes spawns a different film genre: the first, film noir; the second, Mexican soap; the third, absurd-budget anime; the fourth, a documentary about a man who clearly should not have access to heavy technology. The gravity around him behaves like an intern on day one: gets everything wrong but with great confidence.

---

## 😒 What a ridiculous place

Paulo stops at the centre of the auditorium, stares at everyone as if he hated each molecule individually, and says:

> *"What a ridiculous place."*

No one answers. He turns slowly, surveying the entire lab with technical contempt.

> *"So this is it. The great QEL@0xpblab. The sanctuary of science. The temple of causality. The premium butcher shop of logic. The daycare for bureaucrats of the infinite."*

Pablo crosses his arms.

> *"Paulo."*

Paulo whips his head around with hatred accumulated across 9,800 parallel universes.

> *"Don’t say my name like we’re panel colleagues."*  
> *"You invaded my dimension."*  
> *"Correction: I came to collect on a historical injustice."*  
> *"You tried to open a tear in time using a pirate decoder."*  
> *"And it worked partially."*  
> *"Partially blew up four moons."*  
> *"Technical details."*

---

## 📺 The grievance: interdimensional streaming

He points a trembling, accusatory, theatrical, completely unregulated finger.

> *"YOU DENIED ME ACCESS TO YOUR INTERDIMENSIONAL STREAMING SUBSCRIPTIONS."*

Absolute silence. A technician at the back murmurs:

> *"My God. That’s why."*

Paulo turns to him.

> *"YES, THAT’S WHY."*

He now points at everyone with the indignation of a prophet who lost his email password.

> *"You think that’s normal? Fair? Reasonable that a man of my metaphysical magnitude has to watch the collapse of empires in bad resolution? That I have to wait two lunar cycles for a new season of a show that in another dimension has already been cancelled, rebooted, remade in claymation and adapted for chamber opera?"*

He takes another step.

> *"I asked for a password."*  
> *"ONE."*  
> *"SINGLE."*  
> *"MISERABLE."*  
> *"PASSWORD."*

With each word, the projectors blow one by one. In the background the mechanical parrot screeches:

> *"Subscription expired. Subscription expired. Subscription expired."*

Pablo stays still.

> *"You wanted unrestricted access to the catalogue of nineteen four-dimensional platforms, including content that hasn’t been filmed yet."*

Paulo spreads his arms.

> *"So what?"*  
> *"So you have no self-control."*  
> *"Self-control is the religion of the mediocre."*  
> *"You pirated an entire universe just to binge a series you didn’t even like."*

Paulo points violently.

> *"I DIDN’T LIKE IT BECAUSE THE CURATION OF THE THIRD SEASON WAS COWARDLY."*

A doctor from the temporal semiotics wing, unintentionally, nods. Paulo looks at her.

> *"You. You get it."*

She immediately backs away.

> *"No, sorry, reflex."*

---

## ⚔️ I declare the end of this dimension

Paulo smiles. It’s a terrible smile. A smile that looks like a moral rendering error.

> *"Very well. Since Pablo Mu-R4d refused my legitimate access, my divine right, my interstellar audiovisual comfort…"*

He raises his arms to the ceiling.

> *"…I DECLARE THE END OF THIS DIMENSION."*

Nothing happens. Paulo keeps his arms raised. Still nothing. He looks up, irritated.

> *"Where’s my army’s entrance?"*

An awkward silence.

---

## 🛒 The army of nobody

In the background a side door opens by itself. In comes a **shopping cart wheel**. Then another. Then a **market trolley** missing one wheel. Then a **crooked hand-written banner**:

```txt
PAULO PA-MONHA'S POOR ARMY
"NO SOLDIERS, LOTS OF ATTITUDE"
```

And that’s it. **That’s the army.** There are no soldiers. No troops. No infantry. No cavalry. Not even one minimally willing person. There is only the administrative concept of an army, delivered with very poor execution.

A crisis advisor whispers:

> *"He came with no one."*

Another replies:

> *"No. Worse. He came with the idea that he had someone."*

Paulo, with zero shame, points at the void behind him as if commanding legions.

> *"Behold! My devastators! My hounds of ruin! My heralds of the final collapse!"*

Nothing. A plastic bag flies across the room and gets stuck on the chandelier. Paulo continues, unflinching.

> *"They are silent because they were trained in the art of invisible intimidation."*

Pablo pinches the bridge of his nose.

> *"Paulo. You don’t have an army."*  
> *"I do."*  
> *"You don’t."*  
> *"I do."*  
> *"There’s no one there."*  
> *"That’s called military minimalism."*  
> *"That’s called delusion."*  
> *"That’s called asymmetric warfare on a very low budget."*

He kicks the market trolley. The trolley falls apart. A rotten carrot hits the floor with dramatic weight, like civilisation’s last tear. Paulo takes a deep breath, recomposes his posture and decides to double down.

> *"It doesn’t matter. I ALONE AM ENOUGH."*

---

## 📱 The colossal remote control

Then he pulls from inside his overcoat an **impossible artefact**: a **colossal remote control**. Half imperial sceptre, half karaoke accessory stolen from a themed bar, half ergonomic abomination. Yes, three halves. Maths has already given up on this scene.

On the surface of the remote are buttons no language should allow:

| Button | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **PAUSE MATTER** | Time in the east wing gets stuck in a three-second loop: a guard sneezes forever, a cup falls without ever hitting the floor, and a physicist repeats "I knew it" so many times it stops being information and becomes experimental music. |
| **REWIND CONSEQUENCES** | Everyone in the room remembers a lunch that hasn’t happened yet and already regrets it. |
| **MUTE DIPLOMACY** | An Andromeda diplomat opens his mouth and only sad saxophone comes out. |
| **SUBTITLES: [TURN ON DISGRACE]** | — |

Paulo guffaws.

> *"See? I’m the bug the system was missing!"*

The camera circles him. The lights flash red, blue, purple and a legally indescribable shade called *"offensive beige"*. From the ceiling begin to rain old contracts, revoked passwords, expired coupons and cassettes with names like *"Lesson 7 - Cosmic Domination Without Charisma"* and *"Breathing for Villains with Reflux"*.

---

## 🛑 Last chance and the honest password

Pablo takes a step forward.

> *"Last chance. Turn it off and leave."*

Paulo gets close enough for the whole auditorium to smell temporal dust, burnt plastic and vengeance grown in a jar. He speaks quietly. Worse than shouting.

> *"You humiliated me, Pablo. You closed the gates of infinite entertainment in my face. You denied me quantum comedy, ontological drama, reality shows of failed gods and the premium channel that shows films that will be banned next week."*

Closer still.

> *"I had to live like an animal."*

Pablo raises an eyebrow.

> *"You tried to change my password to '1234'."*  
> *"IT WAS AN HONEST PASSWORD."*  
> *"You typed it all in caps."*  
> *"BECAUSE I WAS FEELING A LOT."*

---

## 💥 The central button and the catastrophe

Paulo steps back and turns with volcanic theatricality.

> *"No more crumbs! No more limited catalogues! No more depending on the aesthetic taste of transcendent billionaires and emotionally illiterate algorithms!"*

He aims the remote at the lab’s energy heart.

> *"If I can’t watch everything…"*

A lightning bolt cuts through the room.

> *"…NO ONE ELSE WATCHES ANYTHING. EVER."*

He presses the **central button**. The nameless button. The black button. The button that looks like it was made from the same substance as bad feelings and terms of use written in 8pt font.

The entire lab groans. Not with pain. With **exhaustion**. As if reality itself had realised who was at the controls. Somewhere in the building a coffee machine goes into prophetic mode and starts serving boiling espresso in the shape of small maps of ruin.

The windows show impossible landscapes: an infinite road made of broken remotes; a forest where every tree has a blue screen; a sea of VHS tapes rewinding by themselves; a futuristic city whose entire population is stuck choosing what to watch and never chooses anything. The structure of QEL@0xpblab trembles. The columns turn translucent. The corridors rearrange into the shape of a lazy maze. The sound system fires, without command, a track that sounds like a mix of apocalyptic choir, mattress ad and expensive series season finale.

Pablo looks around. For the first time, truly serious.

> *"You’re not just trying to hit me."*

Paulo smiles again, more crooked, deeper, sicker.

> *"Finally you understand."*

He opens his arms to the catastrophe in progress like a conductor who hates music, audience, instruments and acoustics.

> *"I didn’t come to destroy your lab."*

The lights go out. They turn on behind him, turning his silhouette into a blot of cosmic disrespect.

> *"I came to destroy the entire dimension."*

## 🕳️ The crack and the future Paulo

A **crack** appears in the air, right behind Paulo. It doesn’t look like a portal. It looks like **bad manners in the structure of the universe**. Inside it, echoes of bad futures: cities in ruins; satellites chewed by statistical worms; libraries turned into food courts; empires defeated by misread contracts; continents folded like promo flyers.

And at the centre of it all, on a **throne made of cancelled subscriptions and cracked monitors**, a future version of Paulo Pa-Monha. Bigger. Worse. Triumphant. **The future villain of the lore.** The present Paulo sees his own future reflection and, for the first time, falls silent. Not from fear. From **narcissistic admiration**.

> *"Look at me,"* he whispers. *"I turned out great."*

Pablo realises before anyone else. The [A-Temporal DISRUPTION](/disruptura-atemporal) didn’t end. **It opened the way.** Paulo isn’t just another opportunistic nutcase. He’s a **narrative infection point**. A **walking glitch**. A man so absurdly resentful, so stupidly persistent and so offensively petty that he managed to turn resentment over denied streaming into an eschatological engine of dimensional destruction. In plain language: **a very dangerous idiot.**

The alarms wail. On the main screen new words appear, trembling:

```txt
THREAT CONFIRMED
PAULO PA-MONHA
STATUS: UNACCEPTABLE FUTURE IN HUMAN FORM
```

---

## 👋 Remember this face

Paulo raises the remote like a sword before the end of worlds.

> *"Remember this face."*

He spits on the floor. No reason. Just because.

> *"Remember this name."*

He thumps his chest.

> *"PAULO PA-MONHA."*

The mechanical parrot vibrates and shouts:

> *"Incorrect password!"*

Paulo laughs as if he’d invented evil.

> *"Today I came alone."*

He looks at the crooked banner of the non-existent army.

> *"…more or less."*

He kicks the shopping cart wheel away.

> *"But tomorrow I’ll be back with hunger, with hatred and with a force capable of wrenching this dimension off the rails and selling it for parts."*

He stares at Pablo.

> *"And when I come back, I won’t ask for a password."*

The crack behind him pulses. The whole room seems to hold its breath.

> *"I’ll take the catalogue of reality."*

---

## 🌑 Disappearance and hook

Paulo takes a step back and **disappears** into the crack, along with the parrot, the mediocre banner and the night’s residual dignity. The crack closes with the dry sound of an old TV switching off.

Silence. Too long. Finally the telepathic fish speaks, horrified:

> *"I hate to admit it, but that was a memorable entrance."*

Pablo keeps looking at the void where Paulo was.

> *"Yes."*  
> *"Is he coming back?"*  
> *"Yes."*  
> *"With an army?"*  
> *"Not necessarily."*

The camera slowly pulls back through the devastated auditorium. Burned monitors. Documents in cold flames. A rotten carrot at the centre of the chaos. On the screen, the last frozen image before the system went down completely:

```txt
PAULO PA-MONHA — FIRST RECORDED INCURSION
MOTIVATION: VENGEANCE FOR DENIED ACCESS TO INTERDIMENSIONAL STREAMING SUBSCRIPTIONS
STRATEGIC ASSESSMENT: RIDICULOUS, BUT UNFORTUNATELY SERIOUS
```

Cut to black. But before the total end, a voice echoes from the darkness, distant, furious, with zero composure:

> *"AND ANOTHER THING: THAT SHOW’S FINALE WAS COWARDLY."*

---

## 📜 Official lore hook

From that night on, the name **Paulo Pa-Monha** began to circulate through the corridors of QEL@0xpblab like plague, prophecy and labour lawsuit all at once. He wasn’t just an invader. He was the **first documented case of villainy driven by catalogue resentment**, a psycho-audiovisual force so petty it could tear ontology itself apart out of spite. And the worst of it: the [A-Temporal DISRUPTION](/disruptura-atemporal) had shown an intolerable truth — in some possible future, Paulo would stop being just a cosmic clown and become a **real, central, recurring and stupidly hard to eliminate threat**.
