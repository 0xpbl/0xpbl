## 🌤️ Prologue: what was the Quantumheaven Pit

The **Quantumheaven Pit** was not, technically, a pit.

It was a vertical flaw in reality, a metaphysical cylinder where:

* dreams gained echo,
* photons worked overtime,
* and anyone who looked inside had the impression the bottom was saying:

> *"come see something real quick."*

That was, historically, the worst phrase the universe ever thought.

The Pit was below Wing 7, between the Dawn Room and the corridor that smelled of reheated coffee, tired lamp, and poorly documented decisions. Some said it was a shortcut to quantum paradise. Others said it was a *Hell* leak with a startup budget. In practice, it was an unresolved cross between:

* the luminous arrogance of **Icarus**, who flew too close to the Sun and fell; ([britannica.com](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Icarus-Greek-mythology))
* the circular descent of **Dante's Inferno**, where everyone learns too late; ([britannica.com](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Inferno))
* and the doomed-duo energy of **Thelma & Louise**, except instead of a car there was a loose handrail and terrible decisions. ([britannica.com](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Thelma-and-Louise))

The QEL, for reasons no one tries to justify anymore, had a sign next to it that read:

```txt
QUANTUMHEAVEN PIT
DO NOT LOOK FOR MORE THAN 3 SECONDS
DO NOT PHILOSOPHIZE AT THE EDGE
DO NOT POINT LASER AT THE BOTTOM
```

Nikols broke all three rules.
[Albino Bino](/villains) upgraded the offense.

## 💤 Why Nikols ended up there

Nikols was having a terrible week, which for him meant:

* he was sleeping as a defense mechanism,
* waking up as an offense,
* and writing in a notebook phrases like:

  * *"sleep is the only cloud that doesn't ask for a status update"*
  * *"my pillow understands me more than the committee"*

His wife hated him with consistency.
His mother hated him with infrastructure.
The whiteboard considered him unproductive in multiple languages.

Then he heard about the Quantumheaven Pit.

They told him:

> *"Down there, dreams get more vivid."*

That was all Nikols needed to hear to completely ignore the rest of the sentence, which was:

> *"…and the chance of you coming back normal is statistically pathetic."*

Nikols saw in the Pit a chance to finally prove that:

1. dream was work;
2. REM was method;
3. and real life could, with some effort and little ethics, be postponed.

He packed a bag with:

* a small pillow,
* two notebooks,
* a pen that didn't work when confronted,
* and an expired cereal bar he called "hope reserve."

When he reached the edge, he said with the enthusiasm of someone who doesn't notice the literary genre they've stepped into:

> *"If I fall asleep on the way down, technically I'm already arriving while researching."*

The whiteboard, seven meters away, wrote by itself:

```txt
NO
```

Nikols interpreted it as concrete poetry.

## ✨ Why Doktor Albino Bino went too

[Doktor Albino Bino](/villains) was not interested in the Pit for spirituality.
He was interested in the **reflection**.

There were rumors that at the bottom of Quantumheaven, light no longer obeyed common physics. There were tired photons, indecisive particles, and a residual glow so indecent that even a mirror could leave convinced it had a soul.

For a man who had already tried to steal the light with:

* a funnel,
* a lead helmet,
* and too much self-esteem,

that sounded like vacation.

Albino Bino decided the Pit was his chance to become:

> *whiter still.*

No one understood exactly what that meant.
That was never an obstacle for him.

He brought:

* an optical pointer,
* three pocket mirrors,
* a surgical magnifying glass,
* and a form titled **PROTOCOL FOR LUMINOUS ASCENSION**.

When he saw Nikols at the edge, Albino Bino reacted like every serious expert facing a disaster in the making:

— *What are you doing here?*

Nikols replied:

— *I came to dream deeper.*

Albino retorted:

— *I came to shine higher.*

They stayed silent for two seconds.

Then Nikols asked:

— *Do you also feel like this pit understands you?*

Albino replied:

— *No. I feel like it reflects me.*

It was the most honest and most worrying conversation of the week.

## 🪜 The descent (or: the worst field trip in QEL history)

[Jão Bolão](/jao-bolao), against every ethical impulse, had installed a metal ladder next to the Pit with an additional sign:

```txt
USE AT YOUR OWN RISK
IF YOU DIE, DIE WITH POSTURE
```

[Old Ed](/old-ed) was notified.
Too old to run and too sensible to approve, he showed up only to say:

— *Don't go down.*

Nikols replied:

— *I need to.*

Albino Bino replied:

— *I deserve to.*

Old Ed stamped the air, which in his language meant:

> **you both deserve supervision.**

But they were already climbing down.

With every meter:

* Nikols grew more philosophical,
* Albino grew more dazzled,
* and the ladder made sounds that, in any other context, would be classified as a cry for help.

On the fifth level, Nikols began to see images of himself in possible branches:

* Nikols employed,
* Nikols punctual,
* Nikols loved without effort,
* Nikols sleepy yet respected.

It shook him deeply.

On the sixth level, Albino began to see optical versions of himself:

* Albino white,
* Albino whiter,
* Albino absurdly white,
* Albino so bright he turned into a spreadsheet.

It made him too arrogant to keep his center of gravity.

[Digo Drogado](/xerige-digo), up top, watching the scene with the tired clarity of someone who understood too early, murmured:

— *This has Icarus-with-insomnia energy.*

[Marcelo Marmelo Martelo](/marcelo) replied:

— *I said it was dangerous.*

No one gained anything from that remark. But it was correct.

## 💥 The exact moment of tragedy

The problem started small, like all truly destructive corporate problems.

Nikols stopped halfway down and decided to lie on a side platform "just for 40 strategic seconds."

Albino, seeing the light reflect off his forehead, interpreted it as a mystical sign.

— *You're emitting.*

Nikols, already half in REM, replied:

— *I've always emitted… I was just misunderstood.*

Albino then had the worst idea of his entire career, and that includes the photonic funnel.

He took one of the mirrors, pointed the pit's light at Nikols's face, and said:

— *If I align the glow of the dream with the reflection of the skull, I might get a definitive white.*

Nikols opened one eye, enough to contribute to his own end:

— *If you do it in 7/8, maybe it opens an emotional shortcut.*

In any normal company, that sentence would have ended the experiment.
At the QEL, it worked as formal authorization.

Albino adjusted the mirror.
Nikols adjusted the pillow.
The light hit.
The dream reacted.
The ladder got it wrong.

And then it happened.

It wasn't an explosion.
It was worse.

It was a **slippage of meaning**.

The side platform lost its own conviction, the metal decided to stop participating in the gravitational argument, and the two began to fall together, in a way so choreographed that Digo said under his breath:

— *There. Thelma & Louise with a handrail.*

Down they went:

* Nikols falling as someone who still believed the fall could be turned into metaphor;
* Albino falling as someone who thought in the last second he could still steal the light from his own tragedy.

During the fall, the Pit showed things.

To Nikols:

* his wife not hating him for 12 seconds;
* his mother saying "I'm sorry, my son" in a highly unlikely branch;
* a bed where no one demanded productivity;
* and Diablo II running smooth on [Willy](/willy-bebe)'s childhood computer, which was emotionally dishonest.

To Albino:

* a white so pure there was no shadow;
* a perfect surgery;
* a mirror finally satisfied;
* and snow asking for aesthetic advice.

Neither of them slowed down.

Because the Quantumheaven Pit was, above all, a pedagogical mechanism with zero pedagogy.

## 🗣️ The absolutely wrong last words

Witnesses disagree.

But the most accepted consensus is this:

Nikols, falling, is said to have said:

> *"If I die asleep, technically I rest professionally."*

Albino Bino, spinning beside him, holding a mirror like someone holding expired dignity, is said to have replied:

> *"If I disappear while shining, it counts as an upgrade."*

Then there was a flash.
A silence.
A distant sound of something emotionally costly hitting something structurally poorly installed.

And then… nothing.

No body came back.

Only a notebook floated slowly up with the phrase:

> *"the bottom dreams too"*

And a cracked mirror reflecting more sky than it should.

Then another.
Then stayed five minutes looking without saying anything, which was the emotional equivalent of a collapse.

INCIDENT CLOSED

[Willy](/willy-bebe) felt the room grow cold and, by pure traumatic reflex, tried to heat the lab's guilt by 0.6 degrees.

### Sora.IA

[Sora.IA](/sora-ia) did not cry.
She organized the grief.
Which was much worse.

She decreed:

* silence in the corridor,
* low light in Wing 7,

## 🕯️ Epilogue: when absurdity turns into longing

The QEL kept running, as it always had:

**despite conditions.**

But it was never the same again.

Nikols left a soft void, the kind that makes any chair seem a little more tired. A pillow ghost. An absence in REM.

Albino Bino left an uneasy glow on surfaces, as if every lamp had become a little vainer after him.

Every now and then, in Wing 7:

* a light flickers for no reason;
* someone yawns in the middle of a meeting and feels like philosophizing;
* a mirror reflects too much;
* a report shows up sleepy;
* and the air has that strange feeling that two deeply wrong people may have been, deep down, tragically coherent.

The whiteboard, weeks later, wrote by itself:

> **IT DIDN'T WORK OUT. BUT IT WAS SO THEM.**

[Old Ed](/old-ed) stamped below:

> **CONSISTENT ENOUGH.**

And perhaps that was the most beautiful ceremony that cynical universe ever managed to produce.

Because in the end:

* Nikols fell trying to turn pain into dream,
* Albino fell trying to turn light into possession,
* and both discovered, too late, that the Quantumheaven Pit granted no wish.

It only revealed, with impeccable cruelty, what each one already was.

And still…
it was beautiful.

In a horrible way.
Ridiculous.
Human.
Irreparable.

Very QEL.
