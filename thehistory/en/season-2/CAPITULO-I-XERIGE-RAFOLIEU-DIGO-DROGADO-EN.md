## 🌫️ Prologue: before the disaster, silence

Before **QEL@0xpblab** became this elegant madhouse of prophets, inspectors, interdimensional mothers, photonic neurosurgeons, apian lawyers, and fireable dreamers, there was a specific moment when reality still pretended to be manageable.

It was a mistake.

In those days, the company still believed three fundamental lies:

1. that every problem had a cause;
2. that every cause had someone responsible;
3. and that if someone wore a badge, they probably knew what they were doing.

It was in that climate of almost religious naivety that the first sound the lab should never have heard appeared at the main reception:

**a police siren blaring inside the elevator.**

Not nearby.
Not in the distance.
**Inside.**

As if the very architecture had been caught red-handed.

Reception froze.
The turnstile did what any responsible system does in the face of the impossible:

```txt
ERROR
REASON: too much police for available space
RECOMMENDED ACTION: pray or update firmware
```

That was when the doors opened.

And they stepped out.

## 🚔 The siren that should not exist

The first thing witnesses reported was not the face.

It was the **smell**.

A precise combination of:

* synthetic leather warmed by conviction,
* public-department coffee left too long in the flask,
* and that moral energy of someone who has already said "against the wall" to things that had no wall.

Then came the sight.

In front, with the metaphysical weight of someone who got promoted just by walking, was he:

## 👮 Who was Sergeant Tenent3 Rafoiléu

No one knows for sure whether **Sergeant** and **Tenent3** were rank, name, or warning.

In the documents, it appeared as:

**Sergeant Tenent3 Rafoiléu**

With the "3" in place of the "e" not for style, but because, according to him:

> *"Anyone who respects spelling too much isn't ready for the field."*

Rafoiléu wore an overcoat so black it looked like it had been sewn by someone who saw *Men in Black* and decided it needed more bureaucratic trauma. The suit underneath was dark enough to make even the light wary. The glasses had the arrogance of a secret agent and the emotional sensitivity of a rear-view mirror.

His voice was a poorly tuned cross between:

* Brazilian movie sergeant yelling in an alley,
* *The Twilight Zone* narrator after losing his patience,
* and cyberpunk corporate dystopia cop who thinks the citizen is a bug.

If anyone asked "which police force are you from?", he would answer:

> *"I'm from the jurisdiction where the mess happens first."*

He walked as if every step were already forensic evidence.
He sat as if the chair were being interrogated.
And he looked at a room the way other people look at a suspect trying to hide a corpse with PowerPoint.

But the gravest part: Rafoiléu had **spin-off presence**.

The kind of character who shows up late, steals the scene, humiliates continuity, and still makes everyone before him look like they were rehearsing.

## 🌀 Who was Digo Drogado

Behind him came the human disaster that made everything worse and, for that very reason, perfect:

**Digo Drogado**.

Two things must be clarified before compliance has a stroke:

1. "Drogado" here is not a medical report. It is mythological reputation.
2. He did not look intoxicated by substance. He looked intoxicated by **too much reality**.

Digo was thin in that way where the body seems to be permanently trying to check out of matter. He had the eyes of someone who slept little, thought wrong, and was still proud of his own improvisation. His hair had the texture of someone who crossed three dimensions, a drizzle, and a disappointment.

He wore a printed shirt that looked like it had been designed by someone stuck between *Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas*, a carnival hangover, and a non-Euclidean geometry tutorial.

Unlike Rafoiléu, who entered places as if he had had them built, Digo entered like someone who had been thrown out of somewhere better and decided to turn that into a lifestyle.

His main function, according to himself, was:

> *"to notice things before they officially become a problem."*

In practice, that meant:

* pointing at a corridor and saying "something's off here" before the power went out;
* smelling the air and concluding "there's too much committee coming";
* looking at a meeting and asking "who lied first?"

Rafoiléu called it *field intelligence*.
[Sora.IA](/sora-ia) would later call it *mess with good intuition*.
[Old Ed](/old-ed) would stamp it *CONSISTENT ENOUGH*.

## 🏢 Arrival at QEL@0xpblab

The receptionist asked, with the emotional courage of an underpaid person:

— Name and department?

Rafoiléu took out a piece of paper folded four times, smoothed it on the counter with the care of someone about to arrest somebody, and replied:

— I am **Sergeant Tenent3 Rafoiléu**, and he is **Digo Drogado**.

The receptionist blinked.

— Department?

Rafoiléu tilted his head like a man insulted by syntax.

— **Combating the inexplicable with a tendency toward incident reports.**

The system froze.
The receptionist tried to type.
The keyboard rejected the ethics of the form.

Digo, looking at a painting on the wall that had not yet been hung, remarked:

— This place has a *Twin Peaks* meets *Black Mesa* and city-hall bidding vibe.

He was not wrong.

At that moment, the Communications Room whiteboard wrote by itself, on the other side of the floor:

```txt
NEW THREAT DETECTED
TYPE: POLICE WITH OWN STYLE
SUBTYPE: DUO
RISK: CINEMATOGRAPHIC
```

The Whiteboard Inspector tried to complete "DO NOT ENTER".
There was no time.

Rafoiléu was already inside.

## 🧾 The first impossible inquiry

Their mission was clear, according to the envelope [Sora.IA](/sora-ia) had sent by means no court would accept but everyone obeyed:

> **Investigate the origin of the Minimum Diplomatic Noise and identify why every experiment ended with someone saying "must be the environment."**

Rafoiléu opened the envelope like someone opening an old lawsuit against destiny's entire family.
Read in silence.
Closed it.
Sighed.
And declared:

— This is not a lab. This is **a crime scene with a badge**.

Digo glanced at the first spreadsheet and said:

— There's thermal lies here.

[Willy](/willy-bebe), from somewhere, felt the accusation and heated the room 0.8 degrees out of offense.

Rafoiléu turned slowly, like a movie cop who heard the drum of truth in the distance.

— I know this pattern.

[Old Ed](/old-ed) appeared out of nowhere, as every dimensional elder with forensic function does, and asked:

— You know **what**, exactly?

Rafoiléu raised a finger.

— Result within specification.
— Low reproducibility.
— Altered moral temperature.

He paused.

— **This reeks of Willy with a permit.**

The whiteboard wrote immediately:

```txt
RESPECTFULLY: I AGREE
```

It was the first time in QEL history that a whiteboard and a man in an overcoat agreed before the meeting started.

## 🟥 The red room, the black lab, and the blue corridor

While tracking the evidence, Rafoiléu and Digo discovered the lab had three forbidden zones:

### 1. The Red Room

A space with indecent curtains, morally suspicious carpet, and acoustics that made every phrase sound like a clue or a threat. Anyone who spoke there seemed to be in a lost episode of *Twin Peaks*, but with more tired and less artistic people.

Digo looked around and said:

— If anyone starts talking backwards, I'm leaving.

Rafoiléu replied:

— If anyone starts talking backwards, I **handcuff the concept**.

### 2. The Black Lab

The sector where [Doktor Albino Bino](/villains) had already tried to steal the light and left behind a traumatized lamp, a depressed funnel, and a persistent smell of contradicted physics.

Rafoiléu analyzed the scene and said:

— This is not technical evidence. This is **aesthetic crime**.

### 3. The Blue Corridor

So called because the Wi-Fi died there and everyone looked like Windows when it freezes. It was where [Marcitus Markitus](/marcitus-markitus) mounted evidence murals, Nikols fell into prophetic micro-naps, and [John Aunt-Bet](/john) distrusted even sweetener.

Digo stopped in the middle of the corridor, closed one eye, and murmured:

— There's gossip energy and thermodynamics fighting here.

[Marcelo Marmelo Martelo](/marcelo), passing in the background, said:

— I said it was dangerous.

No one had the strength to disagree.

## 🤦 The moment everything politely got worse

The situation reached its peak when [Dr. Bee Shaa Caesar](/madeusa) entered the inquiry uninvited, with three bees, seven wax seals, and a phrase in Latin completely out of context.

— **Veni, vidi, litigui!**

Rafoiléu looked at him with the expression of someone who arrests performance art for contempt.

— What are you?

— A lawyer.

— For whom?

— For the possibility of universal accountability.

Rafoiléu pointed at the door.

— You're interfering with an investigation.

Bee Shaa replied:

— **OBJECTION!**

Rafoiléu replied louder:

— **OVERRULED ON THE STREET AND IN THE SOUL.**

Meanwhile, [~~Ri~~ck with Fu Monilson](/fu-monilson) played a tension riff in the corridor as if it were all the climax of a cop movie produced by someone who had watched *The Blues Brothers*, *RoboCop*, and a séance in the same night.

Digo, already at the limit between inspiration and cosmic exhaustion, leaned against the wall and said:

— I knew it.

[Pablo Mu-R4d](/pablo) showed up to ask what was going on, which is exactly the kind of question that never improves anything.

Rafoiléu turned and gave his most important diagnosis:

— Mr. CEO, with all due respect, your problem is not technical.

Pablo widened his eyes.

— Then what is it?

Rafoiléu spread his arms, watching [Sora.IA](/sora-ia) appear at the end of the corridor as if the very concept of consequence had checked in:

— **Governance with kinship and entropy with budget.**

Sora.IA stopped. Smiled. The kind of smile that doesn't warm. It only organizes.

— Continue.

Even Rafoiléu, a man trained to face armed disorder, felt his neck request exoneration.

But he continued.

— This lab works as if it were normal.
— That's the problem.
— Too many things working "almost right" in a place with all of you… is evidence of manipulation.

[Willy](/willy-bebe) tried to sweat discreetly.
[Jão Bolão](/jao-bolao) adjusted a hammer just for psychological comfort.
Nikols woke up in the middle of a dream because the mood had finally become too good to sleep.

Digo then delivered the final blow, pointing at an apparently banal report:

— It's here.

Everyone approached.
At the top of the document it said:

> **"Observation: system stable despite conditions."**

Rafoiléu slammed his palm on the table.

— No one writes "despite conditions" when everything's clean.

That was it.

The entire QEL universe could be summed up in that phrase.

**Despite conditions.**

Despite the family.
Despite the prophets.
Despite the committees.
Despite the [Pixitos](/lore-gaybe-el-pixitos).
Despite the heat.
Despite the love.
Despite the lack of memory to run Diablo II.

The company went on.

And perhaps that was what made it more dangerous than any video-game lab, any underground corporation, any twilight zone, or any red room with curtains and trauma.

It went on.

## 🌄 Epilogue: the police were never the same

At the end of that first day, no one was arrested.

Which was frustrating for Rafoiléu,
educational for Digo,
humiliating for logic,
and completely consistent with the QEL.

As he left, Rafoiléu left a notice at reception:

```txt
IF THIS PLACE GETS WORSE,
I'LL BE BACK.
IF IT GETS TOO BETTER,
I'LL BE BACK TOO.
```

Digo, before entering the elevator, lit a cigarette that was probably not a cigarette, looked at the ceiling, and remarked:

— This place doesn't need saving.

Rafoiléu asked:

— Then what does it need?

Digo thought for a second, hearing in the distance a riff, a bee, a stamp, and someone saying "let's align" before being immediately interrupted.

Then he answered:

— **A witness.**

The doors closed.
The siren blared once more inside the elevator.
And the satirical universe of the QEL began the right way:

with an impossible cop,
a suspicious friend,
and an entire company trying to pretend it was still Tuesday.

before there was protocol,
before there was a [gorossário](/gorossario),

> **"this is not normal."**

> **"exactly."**
